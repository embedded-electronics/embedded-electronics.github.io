Frequently Asked Questions

What is Embedded Electronics?
Embedded Electronics is a learning platform that provides tutorials, projects, e-books, and quizzes focused on Embedded Systems, IoT, and Electronics for students and professionals.
What is the Technical IQ Test?
👉 It is an online quiz based on Embedded Systems, Electronics, and related technical topics, designed to test and improve your knowledge.
How can I get a certificate?
👉 You need to take the Technical IQ Test at IQ Test , score 80% or above, and then share your Name, Institute Name, and Score Screenshot on WhatsApp at +91 9547394654.
How often can I participate?
👉 A new question set is available every Sunday. You can participate once per Sunday to qualify for a certificate.
Is there any fee for the certificate?
👉 No, the certificate is completely free for participants who meet the score requirement.
How will I receive my certificate?
👉 Once your details (Your Full Name & Your Institute/College Name) and score screenshot are verified, you will receive a digital certificate via WhatsApp.
Can I get multiple certificates?
👉 Yes, since a new set is uploaded every Sunday, you can participate again and earn multiple certificates over time.
Is the certificate valid for my career/academics?
👉 The certificate is an achievement certificate that highlights your participation and performance. It can be added to your resume, LinkedIn profile, or academic portfolio.
What if I score less than 80%?
👉 You will not be eligible for the certificate. But don’t worry – you can try again next Sunday with the new question set.
How can I take the Typing Test?
Go to the Typing Test page from the navigation menu. Select a difficulty, start the timer, and type the displayed sentence. Results will show your speed and accuracy.
Do you provide free resources?
Yes! We provide free tutorials, projects, and quizzes. You can also access our e-books and practice tests to strengthen your knowledge.
How can I contact you?
Visit our Contact Page where you will find a form, email address, phone number, and WhatsApp support link.
