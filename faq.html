Frequently Asked Questions

What is Embedded Electronics?
👉 Embedded Electronics is a learning platform that provides tutorials, projects, e-books, and quizzes focused on Embedded Systems, IoT, and Electronics for students and professionals.
What is the Free Embedded Systems Certification Program?
👉 It is a 100% free online certification initiative by Embedded Electronics designed to help students and aspiring engineers build strong foundations in Embedded Systems through learning, testing, and certification.
Who can participate in this program?
👉 This program is open to beginners, diploma students, engineering students, and anyone interested in Embedded Systems, programming, and electronics.
Is the certification program really free?
👉 Yes. The program is completely free. There are no registration fees, hidden charges, or payment requirements.
What topics are covered in this certification program?
👉 The program covers six core areas: Linux Shell Scripting, C Programming, C++ Programming, Python Programming, Technical IQ Test, and Technical Quiz focused on Embedded Systems and Electronics fundamentals.
Will I get a certificate for each topic?
👉 Yes. After successfully completing each individual topic, you will receive a Participation Certificate for that specific topic.
What is the Embedded Systems Course Certificate?
👉 Once you successfully complete all six topics, you will be awarded the “Embedded Systems Course Certificate”, recognizing your complete learning journey with Embedded Electronics.
Is there any passing or score requirement?
👉 Yes. You need to meet the minimum qualifying criteria specified for each assessment to be eligible for the corresponding certificate.
Can I complete the topics at my own pace?
👉 Yes. The program is flexible, allowing you to learn, attempt tests, and complete certifications at your own pace.
How will I receive my certificates?
👉 After verification, digital certificates will be shared with you via WhatsApp or the communication method mentioned during registration.
How can I register for this free certification program?
👉 To participate, simply fill out the Google Form provided for registration. Further instructions and updates will be shared after successful registration.
Is the certificate valid for my career/academics?
👉 The certificate is an achievement certificate that highlights your participation and performance. It can be added to your resume, LinkedIn profile, or academic portfolio.
What if I score less than 80%?
👉 You will not be eligible for the certificate. But don’t worry – you can try again next Sunday with the new question set.
How can I take the Typing Test?
👉 Go to the Typing Test page from the navigation menu. Select a difficulty, start the timer, and type the displayed sentence. Results will show your speed and accuracy.
Do you provide free resources?
👉 Yes! We provide free tutorials, projects, and quizzes. You can also access our e-books and practice tests to strengthen your knowledge.
How can I contact you?
👉 Visit our Contact Page where you will find a form, email address, phone number, and WhatsApp support link.
