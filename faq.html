Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Embedded Systems Professional Training Programme?
👉 The Embedded Systems Professional Training Programme is a structured online live training programme by Embedded Electronics, designed for college students and working professionals who want to develop practical and technical skills in Embedded Systems.
When does the October 2026 batch start?
👉 The October 2026 batch will begin on 2 October 2026.
How long is the Professional Training Programme?
👉 The training programme has a duration of 6 months.
Who can join the Professional Training Programme?
👉 The programme is designed for college students and working professionals interested in Embedded Systems and practical electronics.
What is the mode of training?
👉 The training is conducted through online live classes.
What is the course fee?
👉 The Professional Training Programme fee is ₹3,000.
Is an EMI option available?
👉 Yes. A monthly EMI option of ₹550/month is available.
When are the live classes conducted?
👉 Weekdays: Evening
Weekends: Afternoon
What happens if I miss a live class?
👉 Class recordings will be available for later viewing, so you can catch up on missed sessions.
Is this programme self-paced?
👉 The programme follows scheduled online live classes. Recorded classes are available for review or catching up on missed sessions.
What kind of learning approach does the training follow?
👉 The training follows a structured and practical learning approach, supported by live instruction and technical demonstrations.
Can I see demo classes before joining?
👉 Yes. Selected demo classes are available from our Basic Arduino, Advanced Arduino, and PIC16F877A Microcontroller playlists. They can help you understand our teaching style and practical approach.
Where can I find the course syllabus?
👉 The course syllabus is provided with the Professional Training Programme information. You can also contact us if you need clarification about the syllabus.
Do I need to register if I have already completed registration?
👉 No. If you have already completed your registration, no further registration action is required at this time.
How can I register for the Professional Training Programme?
👉 If you are interested in joining and have not yet registered, please complete the Professional Training Registration Form.
Is the Professional Training Programme the same as the earlier free certification programme?
👉 No. The Professional Training Programme is a structured 6-month paid training programme with online live classes. It is different from the earlier free certification initiative.
Do you still provide free learning resources?
👉 Yes. Embedded Electronics continues to provide free tutorials, projects, quizzes, e-books, and other learning resources. These resources are separate from the Professional Training Programme.
Will I receive a certificate after completing the Professional Training Programme?
👉 Certificate details for the Professional Training Programme should be confirmed with Embedded Electronics during registration or through the official course communication.
What if I have questions about payment or registration?
👉 For questions regarding the course, schedule, registration, or payment, please contact Embedded Electronics for assistance.
How can I contact Embedded Electronics?
👉 Visit our Contact Page or email us at info@mbedelectronics.com.
